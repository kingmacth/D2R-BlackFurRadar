◆分阶段流程

加点原则只有一句：先保证恶魔数量和羊头人伤害，再为束缚恶魔的「抓超级暗金」门槛存点。3.2 起数量与抓取门槛只认硬点（自己投的点），装备 +技能不算。

阶段一 · Lv 1–12羊头人起家

路线：普通 A1（邪恶巢穴 → 崔斯特瑞姆救凯恩）→ A2 沙漠。佣兵先用 A1 罗格过渡。

技能加点

  • Lv1：召唤羊头人 1——开局全部输出
  • Lv2–5：恶魔精通 → 5，硬点满 5 解锁第 2 只恶魔
  • Lv6：死亡印记 1——把羊头人直接传送到怪脸上并降防，刷 Boss 神技
  • Lv7–11：羊头人继续加（约到 7–8），有富余先存着等精通
  • Lv12：污染魔 1（过路，往污秽者/束缚方向走）

属性点

  • 力量：先加到 25–35（穿轻甲、拿魔典）
  • 敏捷：不加；精力：不加（喝蓝药过渡）
  • 其余全部活力——这条规则全程不变

装备目标

  • 商店和掉落里淘 +羊头人 / +恶魔技能的魔典（术士副手），加 1 级都是质变
  • 其余部位只看：生命、抗性、跑速，怎么活怎么来

在哪里刷

  • 符文起步：遗忘之塔地窖 5 层女伯爵（黑色荒地），反复刷塔尔（7号）、拉尔（8号）
  • 经验：崔斯特瑞姆、泰摩高地，怪密等级合适
本阶段验收：2 只羊头人 + 死亡印记，安达利尔站后面看戏就行。

阶段二 · Lv 13–24三只成军，羊头人点满

路线：普通 A3 → A4 → A5。17 级做隐密，推图速度明显起飞。

技能加点

  • Lv13–17：恶魔精通 → 10，硬点满 10 解锁第 3 只恶魔——全流派第一个大节点
  • Lv18 前后各 1 点过路：污秽者 1、暴食 1（束缚恶魔的前置链）、血誓 1（给召唤物减伤/保命）、迟缓咒印 1、飞刃传形 1（位移跑图）
  • Lv19–24：召唤羊头人 → 20 点满（含任务奖励点，约 24 级满）

属性点

  • 力量：补到 35–45 够穿隐密甲和后续装备即可
  • 其余全部活力，此时血量应明显厚于同级法系

装备目标

  • 隐密（17 级）：塔尔 7号 + 伊司 5号，2 孔甲——跑速/施法/打击恢复全给
  • 通关 A5 后任务直接送 远古誓言的符文组（拉尔 8号 + 欧特 9号 + 塔尔 7号），做盾补抗

在哪里刷

  • 经验：A5 世界之石要塞刷巴尔小队（普通巴尔 Run）
  • 通关后开普通奶牛关：捡一把水晶剑留着——下阶段做精神用
本阶段验收：3 只满级羊头人。羊头人自带压碎性打击，打 Boss 比你想象中快得多。

阶段三 · Lv 25–30精神出鞘，束缚恶魔解锁

路线：进噩梦 A1–A2。25 级精神剑是整个开荒最大的单件提升。

技能加点

  • Lv25–29：死亡印记补到 8 左右（降防增伤的甜点区），其余存点
  • Lv30：束缚恶魔 1——立刻去抓第一只绑定恶魔
  • 1 点只能抓普通恶魔：先在 A4 火焰之河抓一只顺手的近战恶魔过渡即可。铸甲师海法斯特是超级暗金，要等束缚硬点满 20（阶段四）才能抓——他会放伤害加深，是中期的头号目标，别 30 级就白跑一趟

属性点

  • 力量：补到 55 左右，够精神剑和噩梦期装备
  • 其余全部活力

装备目标

  • 精神剑（25 级）：普通牛场水晶剑 → 拉苏克打孔（必 4 孔）→ 塔尔 7号 + 图尔 10号 + 欧特 9号 + 安姆 11号
  • 知识头（27 级）：欧特 9号 + 索尔 12号，+1 全技能
  • 换 A2 佣兵：噩梦进攻型（力量光环）给全队物理增伤；给他攒 4 孔长柄做眼光

在哪里刷

  • 符文：噩梦女伯爵——索尔（12号）等中级符文主要来源，特有掉落最高到科（18号）
  • 底材：噩梦奶牛关捡 4 孔长柄（或无孔长柄用拉苏克/方块打孔）
本阶段验收：精神剑 + 1 只绑定恶魔。注意绑定恶魔跨房间保留，但角色死亡、恶魔死亡、洗点都会解除，死了要重抓。

阶段四 · Lv 31–49束缚恶魔点满——全流派核心工程

路线：噩梦 A3–A5 通关。这一阶段不做别的，所有点优先喂束缚恶魔。

技能加点

  • 束缚恶魔 → 20 点满（约 49 级，含三难度任务点进度）
  • 为什么必须硬点满：抓取门槛只认硬点——10 点抓头目级、15 点抓暗金、20 点抓超级暗金
  • 沿途里程碑：5/10/15/20 级给绑定恶魔额外强壮、特别快速、幽灵打击、光环附魔等词缀
  • 点满后第一件事：回 A4 火焰之河抓铸甲师海法斯特（会放伤害加深，全队受益）；再往后目标是带狂热/信念/力量光环的超级暗金恶魔（如李斯特一类），一只顶半支军队

属性点

  • 力量：70–90 区间，按身上装备需求随时补
  • 其余全部活力；噩梦抗性惩罚 −40，装备开始优先补抗

装备目标

  • 佣兵眼光（27 级起可做）：拉尔 8号 + 特尔 3号 + 塔尔 7号 + 索尔 12号，4 孔长柄——冥思光环解决全队蓝量
  • 抗性不够就换烟雾甲：内夫 4号 + 卢姆 17号，+50 全抗过渡地狱
  • 继续淘 +恶魔技能魔典、+技能项链/戒指（婚戒、乔丹这类随缘）

在哪里刷

  • 装备起步：噩梦墨菲斯托（A3 憎恨囚牢 3 层），召唤流站桩刷，性价比全噩梦最高
  • 经验：噩梦巴尔 Run 到 45+ 再考虑进地狱
本阶段验收：束缚恶魔 20 硬点。从这一刻起，你抓的每一只超级暗金都是永久编制（不死不掉）。

阶段五 · Lv 50–65进地狱：精通点满，抗性毕业

路线：地狱 A1–A3。地狱抗性惩罚 −100，进门先看面板，火/电抗尽量往正了补。

技能加点

  • 恶魔精通 → 20 点满：全队恶魔的伤害、攻速、命中、移速一起涨，绑定恶魔同样受益
  • 富余点先给红液沸腾 1+或继续死亡印记，按手感来

属性点

  • 分叉点：打算用精神盾（统治者大盾）就把力量往 156 推；不做盾流就停在装备需求
  • 其余全部活力，地狱期目标血量 1000+

装备目标

  • 精神盾：4 孔统治者大盾（牛场/85 区掉落）做第二件精神，主副手双 +2 技能
  • 盯上贺拉森套装部件：凑两件就有 +恶魔技能，是橡树之心之外的毕业方向
  • 3.4 新版钢铁面纱（+2 全技能、物理减伤）只在天梯掉新版——打到先留着，力量需求 192，后期再说

在哪里刷

  • 地狱墨菲斯托：毕业装主产地，贺拉森部件、技能装都从这儿起步
  • 地狱女伯爵：特有掉落最高罗（28号），也是攒中符换高符的稳定来源
  • 85 级区域：地穴、古代通道（无火免怪时友好）、混沌庇难所
本阶段验收：三核心满了两个半（束缚 20 / 精通 20 / 羊头人 20），地狱 A5 能站住。

阶段六 · Lv 66–80活动周冲刺：恐惧地带 + 黑毛

路线：地狱通关杀巴尔 → 恐怖遗产活动全开。这一段的效率是平时的两倍以上，优先把活动吃满。

技能加点

  • 死亡印记 → 20 点满：降防 + 增伤拉满，羊头人压碎性打击跟着受益，是打黑毛的关键技能
  • 之后补血誓 → 12 左右，给绑定恶魔加血上限和全抗

属性点

  • 维持现状：力量够装备（或 156 精神盾），其余活力
  • 三难度属性任务（艾尔·亚兹德药剂 +15、黄金鸟 +60 生命等）别漏

装备目标

  • 攒符文做战争召唤副手（配方见下）：开打前号令 → 体制两吼，全队血蓝上限一起涨
  • 恶魔技能超大护身符（技能板）、术士地狱火炬、毁灭小符——按这个顺序补
  • 佣兵进阶：眼光 → 有条件换高伤武器 + 吸血头（安达利尔的面容）

在哪里刷

  • 活动期杀巴尔后全图变恐惧地带：挑混沌庇难所、世界之石、牛场轮刷
  • 奶牛关常驻（活动期不用维特之腿）：刷底材和高符
  • 黑毛：每 6 小时整点场次，带压碎的羊头人 + 死亡印记硬啃，掉毁灭小符
本阶段验收：地狱通关 + 第一块毁灭到手。赛季初的毁灭在天梯很值钱，自己不用也能换毕业装。

阶段七 · Lv 81+毕业成型与长期刷宝

路线：恐惧地带轮刷 + 85 区 + 交易补缺。召唤流成型后操作趋近于零，适合长期挂机式刷宝。

技能加点

  • 剩余点：暴食补满方向（吞尸给全队回血续航），或按手感补红液沸腾
  • 洗点只有一次刚需场景：想换格挡流/双持流派时，用泰瑞尔洗点或遗忘药剂整体重构

属性点

  • 定稿：力量 = 装备需求（精神盾流 156），敏捷 0，精力 0，活力拉满

装备目标

  • 主手毕业二选一：橡树之心（连枷）或高技能魔典 + 贺拉森套装
  • 副手定稿：战争召唤 + 精神盾，进图先吼再切回主手
  • 谜团不是刚需：死亡印记本身就能把队伍瞬移到怪脸上，符文先紧着 CTA 和橡树

在哪里刷

  • 恐惧地带（常驻轮换）：混沌庇难所、崔凡科、牛场
  • 高符：地狱牛场、混沌庇难所、女伯爵三点一线
打法循环定稿：召唤三羊 + 抓好的绑定恶魔 → 死亡印记点名 → 站后面捡东西。补 buff 顺序永远是战争召唤先号令、后体制。

◆Phase-by-Phase Plan

One rule: secure demon count and Goatman damage first, then save points for Bind Demon's super-unique threshold. Since 3.2, demon count and capture tiers count hard points only — +skills from gear do not count.

Phase 1 · Lv 1–12Goatman Opener

Route: Normal A1 (Den of Evil → rescue Cain in Tristram) → A2 desert. Use an A1 Rogue mercenary for now.

Skills

  • Lv1: Summon Goatman 1 — your entire damage output early
  • Lv2–5: Demon Mastery → 5, 5 hard points unlock the 2nd demon
  • Lv6: Death Mark 1 — teleports Goatmen onto the target and lowers its defense; the boss-killer button
  • Lv7–11: keep raising Goatman (~7–8), save any surplus for Mastery
  • Lv12: Tainted Blood 1 (pathing toward Bind Demon)

Stats

  • Strength: raise to 25–35 first (light armor, grimoire)
  • Dexterity: none; Energy: none (mana potions carry you)
  • Everything else into Vitality — this rule never changes

Gear targets

  • Shop/farm a grimoire with +Goatman / +demon skills (Warlock off-hand) — +1 level is a real power spike
  • Everything else: life, resists, run speed — survive first

Where to farm

  • First runes: The Countess, Forgotten Tower Cellar Lv5 (Black Marsh) — farm Tal (#7) and Ral (#8)
  • XP: Tristram, Tamoe Highland — dense and level-appropriate
Checkpoint: 2 Goatmen + Death Mark. Stand behind them and watch Andariel die.

Phase 2 · Lv 13–24Army of Three, Max Goatman

Route: Normal A3 → A4 → A5. Stealth at 17 makes the run noticeably faster.

Skills

  • Lv13–17: Demon Mastery → 10, 10 hard points unlock the 3rd demon — the build's first big milestone
  • Around Lv18, 1 point each in pathing skills: Defiler 1, Gluttony 1 (Bind Demon prerequisites), Blood Pact 1 (summon survivability), Sigil of Sloth 1, Blade Warp 1 (mobility)
  • Lv19–24: Summon Goatman → 20 maxed (with quest points, done around Lv24)

Stats

  • Strength: up to 35–45, enough for Stealth and upcoming gear
  • Everything else Vitality — you should already feel tankier than other casters

Gear targets

  • Stealth (Lv17): Tal #7 + Eth #5 in 2-socket armor — run speed, FCR, FHR all in one
  • After A5, the quest hands you the Ancient's Pledge rune set (Ral #8 + Ort #9 + Tal #7) — make the shield for resists

Where to farm

  • XP: Worldstone Keep Baal waves (Normal Baal runs)
  • After clearing, open the Normal Cow Level: pick up a Crystal Sword — it becomes your Spirit next phase
Checkpoint: 3 maxed Goatmen. They carry Crushing Blow, so bosses melt far faster than you'd expect.

Phase 3 · Lv 25–30Spirit Out, Bind Demon Unlocked

Route: Nightmare A1–A2. The Lv25 Spirit sword is the single biggest upgrade of the whole leveling run.

Skills

  • Lv25–29: Death Mark to ~8 (the defense-reduction sweet spot), save the rest
  • Lv30: Bind Demon 1 — go capture your first bound demon immediately
  • 1 point captures regular demons only: grab a handy melee demon in the A4 River of Flame for now. The Smith Hephasto is a super unique — he needs Bind at 20 hard points (Phase 4). He casts Amplify Damage and is your #1 mid-game target; don't waste a trip at 30

Stats

  • Strength: up to about 55 for the Spirit sword and Nightmare gear
  • Everything else Vitality

Gear targets

  • Spirit sword (Lv25): Normal Cow Crystal Sword → Larzuk socket quest (guaranteed 4 sockets) → Tal #7 + Thul #10 + Ort #9 + Amn #11
  • Lore helm (Lv27): Ort #9 + Sol #12, +1 all skills
  • Switch to an A2 mercenary: Nightmare Offensive (Might aura) buffs the whole army's physical damage; start saving a 4-socket polearm for his Insight

Where to farm

  • Runes: Nightmare Countess — main source of Sol (#12) and other mid runes; her special drop goes up to Ko (#18)
  • Bases: Nightmare Cows for a 4-socket polearm (or Larzuk / cube socket a plain one)
Checkpoint: Spirit sword + 1 bound demon. The bind persists across games, but your death, the demon's death, or a respec breaks it — re-capture when that happens.

Phase 4 · Lv 31–49Max Bind Demon — the Core Project

Route: clear Nightmare A3–A5. This phase has one job: feed every point to Bind Demon.

Skills

  • Bind Demon → 20 maxed (around Lv49, counting quest points across difficulties)
  • Why hard-max it: capture tiers count hard points only — 10 pts: champion-tier, 15 pts: unique, 20 pts: super unique
  • Milestones at 5/10/15/20 grant the bound demon bonus affixes: Extra Strong, Extra Fast, Spectral Hit, Aura Enchanted, etc.
  • First job after maxing: go back to the A4 River of Flame and capture Hephasto the Smith (casts Amplify Damage — the whole army benefits). Later targets: super uniques with Fanaticism / Conviction / Might auras (Lister-type packs) — one of those is worth half an army

Stats

  • Strength: 70–90 range, top up as gear demands
  • Everything else Vitality; Nightmare resist penalty is −40, start prioritizing resists on gear

Gear targets

  • Mercenary Insight (makeable from Lv27): Ral #8 + Tir #3 + Tal #7 + Sol #12 in a 4-socket polearm — Meditation aura ends the army's mana problems
  • Short on resists? Switch to a Smoke armor: Nef #4 + Lum #17, +50 all resists into Hell
  • Keep shopping +demon-skill grimoires and +skills amulets/rings (Bul-Kathos, Stone of Jordan if they drop)

Where to farm

  • Gear start: Nightmare Mephisto (A3 Durance of Hate Lv3) — summoners farm him standing still; best value in all of Nightmare
  • XP: Nightmare Baal runs to 45+ before attempting Hell
Checkpoint: Bind Demon at 20 hard points. From now on, every super unique you capture is permanent roster (until it dies).

Phase 5 · Lv 50–65Into Hell: Max Mastery, Graduate Resists

Route: Hell A1–A3. Hell resist penalty is −100 — check your panel before stepping out.

Skills

  • Demon Mastery → 20 maxed: damage, attack speed, AR and move speed for every demon — the bound demon included
  • Surplus points: Boiling Blood 1+ or more Death Mark, to taste

Stats

  • The fork: if you plan a Spirit shield (Monarch), push Strength toward 156; otherwise stop at gear requirements
  • Everything else Vitality; Hell target: 1000+ life

Gear targets

  • Spirit shield: 4-socket Monarch (Cows / lvl-85 areas) — a second Spirit, +2 skills in both hands
  • Watch for Horazon set pieces: two pieces already grant +demon skills — the graduation path besides Heart of the Oak
  • The reworked 3.4 Veil of Steel (+2 all skills, physical damage reduction) drops in its new version on Ladder only — keep one if it drops; 192 Strength requirement, a late-game piece

Where to farm

  • Hell Mephisto: the graduation-gear factory — Horazon pieces and skill gear start here
  • Hell Countess: special drop up to Lo (#28), and the steadiest mid-rune income for trading up
  • Lvl-85 areas: The Pit, Ancient Tunnels (friendly when no fire immunes), Chaos Sanctuary
Checkpoint: two and a half of the three cores maxed (Bind 20 / Mastery 20 / Goatman 20) and able to stand in Hell A5.

Phase 6 · Lv 66–80Event Sprint: Terror Zones + Uber Diablo

Route: clear Hell, kill Baal → the Legacy of Terror event fully opens. Efficiency here is more than double the normal rate — eat the event first.

Skills

  • Death Mark → 20 maxed: full defense reduction + damage amp; Goatman Crushing Blow scales with it — the key skill for Uber Diablo
  • Then Blood Pact → ~12 for the bound demon's life cap and all resists

Stats

  • Hold course: Strength for gear (or 156 for Spirit shield), rest Vitality
  • Don't skip the stat quests in all three difficulties (Lam Esen's Tome +15, Golden Bird +60 life, etc.)

Gear targets

  • Save runes for a Call to Arms off-hand (recipe below): Battle Command first, then Battle Orders — whole army's life/mana caps jump
  • Demon-skill grand charms (skillers), a Warlock Hellfire Torch, and an Annihilus — in that order
  • Mercenary upgrade: Insight → a high-damage weapon + life-leech helm (Andariel's Visage) when affordable

Where to farm

  • During the event, killing Baal turns every zone in that difficulty into a Terror Zone: rotate Chaos Sanctuary, Worldstone, Cows
  • Cow portal stays open during the event (no Wirt's Leg needed): bases and high runes
  • Uber Diablo: guaranteed spawn every 6 hours — Crushing Blow Goatmen + Death Mark grind him down for an Annihilus
Checkpoint: Hell cleared + first Annihilus in hand. Early-season Annihilus is very valuable on Ladder — even a spare one trades for graduation gear.

Phase 7 · Lv 81+Build Complete, Long-Term Farming

Route: Terror Zone rotation + lvl-85 areas + trade for missing pieces. A finished summoner needs near-zero inputs — ideal for relaxed long sessions.

Skills

  • Remaining points: max out Gluttony (corpse-eating sustains the whole army) or Boiling Blood, to taste
  • Only one real respec case: switching to a block build / different variant — use Tyrael's respec or a Token of Absolution for a full rebuild

Stats

  • Final: Strength = gear requirement (156 for the Spirit-shield route), Dexterity 0, Energy 0, Vitality maxed

Gear targets

  • Main hand, pick one graduation path: Heart of the Oak (flail) or a high-skill grimoire + Horazon set
  • Off-hand locked: Call to Arms + Spirit shield — buff on entry, swap back to main
  • Enigma is not a priority: Death Mark already teleports the whole army onto the target. Spend runes on CTA and HotO first

Where to farm

  • Terror Zones (regular rotation): Chaos Sanctuary, Travincal, Cows
  • High runes: Hell Cows, Chaos Sanctuary, Countess — one loop, three stops
Final loop: summon 3 Goatmen + your captured bound demon → Death Mark the target → stand back and pick up loot. Buff order is always CTA Battle Command first, Battle Orders second.

◆Key Runewords (in order of appearance)

Runes are labeled with their numbers. The base must be a plain (grey-text) item with exactly the right socket count — magic items with sockets cannot make runewords, and one rune out of order ruins it.

Stealth Lv 17 · 2-socket armor

Tal#7+Eth#5

Run speed + FCR + FHR. Your first real item — wear it into Nightmare.

Get it: farm Tal/Eth from the Countess, buy a 2-socket light armor in A2

Spirit Lv 25 · 4-socket sword / shield

Tal#7+Thul#10+Ort#9+Amn#11

+2 all skills, big FCR, huge mana. Sword first; Monarch shield version in Hell — +2 in both hands.

Get it: Normal Cow Crystal Sword → Larzuk sockets (guaranteed 4); runes from the Countess

Lore Lv 27 · 2-socket helm

Ort#9+Sol#12

The cheapest +1 skills helm, with lightning resist and mana per kill. Wear until a skill helm / Horazon drops.

Get it: Sol mainly from Nightmare Countess

Insight Lv 27 · 4-socket polearm (merc)

Ral#8+Tir#3+Tal#7+Sol#12

For the A2 merc: Meditation aura refills everyone's mana — summoners stop drinking blue potions. Make two, keep one for a better base.

Get it: 4-socket polearm from Nightmare Cows / road drops, or Larzuk

Ancient's Pledge Lv 21 · 3-socket shield

Ral#8+Ort#9+Tal#7

Big all-resist shield — the rescue item when Hell resists fall apart at the door.

Get it: the rune set is handed to you directly by the A5 quest “Rescue on Mount Arreat” (Qual-Kehk gives Ral + Ort + Tal)

Smoke Lv 37 · 2-socket armor

Nef#4+Lum#17

+50 all resists. The steadiest pre-Hell transition armor — swap from Stealth when Stealth can't keep you alive.

Get it: Lum from Nightmare Countess (inside her special drop range)

Call to Arms Lv 51 · 5-socket weapon (off-hand)

Amn#11+Ral#8+Mal#23+Ist#24+Ohm#27

Off-hand warcries: Battle Command first (all skills +1), then Battle Orders (life/mana/stamina caps) — order matters. Judged by its Battle Orders roll; +6 is the expensive one.

Get it: Ohm from Hell Cows / Chaos Sanctuary or trade; Crystal Sword base keeps it simple

Heart of the Oak Lv 55 · 4-socket flail

Ko#18+Vex#26+Pul#21+Thul#10

+3 all skills, 40 FCR, +40 all resists. One of the two graduation main-hands — pick this or the Horazon set route, not both.

Get it: Vex from Hell Cows / Terror Zones, or cube up / trade

Fortitude Lv 59 · 4-socket armor (merc)

El#1+Sol#12+Dol#14+Lo#28

Mercenary graduation armor: 300% enhanced damage. Lo (#28) is the priciest tile on this page — dress the merc first; Smoke / Horazon can cover you meanwhile.

Get it: Lo at the top of Hell Countess's special drop, Cows, or trade
